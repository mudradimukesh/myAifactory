# Development factory

**Development factory**: The system that develops software changes, verifies them independently, and prepares a handoff to a named recipient.

**Candidate**: An exact submitted software revision awaiting or carrying verification evidence.

**Attempt**: One bounded worker invocation assigned a responsibility and an exact starting revision.

**Verification evidence**: Recorded observations connecting a candidate and approved requirements to executed checks and independent review.

**Handoff**: The candidate, artifact, evidence, limitations, and recovery instructions supplied to the delivery recipient. A handoff does not authorize release.
