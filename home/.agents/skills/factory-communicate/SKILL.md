---
name: factory-communicate
description: Write factory coordination prose and handoffs in plain language while preserving raw evidence and structured records.
---

Use plain, complete sentences and the approved domain vocabulary. State the concrete outcome, mechanism, number, or limitation. Separate observed facts from hypotheses and link evidence to the candidate or task it supports.

Cut filler, praise, vague attribution, abstract metaphors, forced groups of three, and contrast formulas such as "not just X but Y". Prefer active voice. Avoid em dashes, decorative headings, exaggerated adjectives, and fragment chains. Do not replace a precise technical or domain term with a loose synonym.

Handoffs identify completed work, unresolved failures, exact evidence references, and the next observable completion criterion. Keep them short enough to reconstruct the task without repeating logs.

Apply prose edits only to prose. Preserve commands, structured records, quotations, identifiers, and raw evidence unchanged. Do not strengthen a claim beyond its evidence or turn an unexecuted check into a reported pass.

Tailor handoffs to the next assigned responsibility. Include the task and candidate identifiers, outcome, evidence paths, unresolved failures or decisions, and next action. Reference existing briefs, ADRs, diffs, and logs instead of copying them. Name the approved role bundle the next invocation needs without importing unrelated skills. Keep secrets and unnecessary personal data out of prose handoffs; retain original evidence only in its authorized storage.
