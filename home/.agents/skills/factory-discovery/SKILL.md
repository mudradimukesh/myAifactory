---
name: factory-discovery
description: Propose approved-scope user journeys, acceptance examples, or domain rules for a factory business or domain task.
---

Read the assigned task, approved brief, glossary, and relevant evidence. Work in the responsibility named by the task contract.

For a business task, describe the user journey, observable outcome, failure cases, and acceptance examples. For a domain task, define terms, business rules, ambiguous cases, and scenarios. Distinguish observed behavior from intended behavior and unresolved assumptions.

Write proposals only to the assigned scratch directory. Preserve approved documents. Send consequential unresolved product choices and requests for specialists to Coordinator. Decide routine technical details within the delegated task. Do not dispatch other agents.

Return proposed artifact paths, supporting evidence, unresolved decisions, and the next observable acceptance condition. Missing evidence stays unknown; do not invent product requirements.

Keep the handoff specific to the assigned role. Business proposals map each journey to observable acceptance examples. Domain proposals map each rule to glossary terms, source evidence, and ambiguous scenarios. Reference the proposed artifacts rather than repeating their full text in the handoff.
